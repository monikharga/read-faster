Treat any Gen 6 firewall still in service as a high-risk exposure, and migrate to a supported Gen 8 firewall running SonicOS 8." The affected products running SonicOS 6.x include - In recent years, threat actors have increasingly targeted edge devices as a launch point to obtain initial access, even as the window between disclosure and confirmed exploitation has collapsed significantly, in part driven by threat actors' use of automation and AI to scan the internet for unsupported devices, analyze patches and vulnerability disclosure announcements, and build working exploits faster. "Because these assets provide critical inspection of data ingress and egress points, they grant significant visibility and permissions to those who gain access to them." Cisco Talos' 2025 Year in Review report has also found that 23% of CVEs discovered in the year directly impact network devices like VPN appliances, next-generation firewalls (NGFWs), load balancers, routers, and others.
