These data centre companies, known as neocloud firms, run large warehouses packed with expensive computers, which they lease to major AI developers like Anthropic, OpenAI and Meta. Many question whether neocloud firms are financially sound and what their recent stumbles tell us about the state of the AI economy. On Thursday, Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent tried to downplay recent concerns about neocloud firms, calling them "a small component of the overall ecosystem, external" on CNBC.

Neocloud firms often finance themselves by signing long-term lease contracts with major tech customers who need quick access to computing power. Shares in other neocloud firms like Nebius, IREN, and Applied Digital have all shed about that much if not more during the same period. Debt investors also appear spooked, and are demanding ever-higher returns from several firms in the sector.
