The cluster, dubbed O-UNC-053, is characterized by the use of voice phishing (aka vishing) attacks to trick targets into entering credentials in phishing pages and approving multi-factor authentication (MFA). The identity and access management provider said targets of this cluster previously had stolen data published to the ShinyHunters data leak site (DLS). However, as of November 16, 2026, a new data leak site operating under the name INCEPTION has listed the data siphoned from O-UNC-053 targets.

"If successful, the operators gain access to SaaS applications and exfiltrate sensitive data, which is often used to extort targets under the threat of releasing it on a data leak site." Domains created by the group are registered through NiceNIC, Spaceship, Tucows, and NameSilo, with the domain names using the following patterns - The use of "legal" themes in these attacks is said to have started in October 2026, and is often registered on or within a day of a campaign starting.
