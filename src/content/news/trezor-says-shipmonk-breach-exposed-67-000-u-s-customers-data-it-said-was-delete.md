The exposed information includes customer names, email addresses, phone numbers, shipping addresses, and order numbers between November 2019 and August 2021. "Throughout our entire relationship with ShipMonk, we repeatedly requested and received written assurance confirming the deletion of the data, in line with our contract, data policy, and past communications," Trezor said. "We are very disappointed that, despite receiving this confirmation, the data was not deleted in their systems." The exposure is in addition to 13,689 customers the company disclosed last month as having had their data either fully or partially exposed.

Trezor said ShipMonk informed the hardware wallet maker of a breach on August 10, 2026, following unauthorized access to their systems. Subsequently, it revealed that the 1,947 customers whose exposure was limited only to names, cities, and email addresses (excluding the shipping addresses) may include older orders.
