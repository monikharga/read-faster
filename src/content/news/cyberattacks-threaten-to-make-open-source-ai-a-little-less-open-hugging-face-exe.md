The very qualities that make open AI platforms valuable, like open doors and rich data, might also make them vulnerable to more hacks, Irene Solaiman, chief policy officer and SVP of strategy at Hugging Face said on a recent episode of the Equity podcast. "The things I worry about making an open community targeted are also what makes it special," Solaiman said on Equity. The wealth of information open platforms host also make them targets.

Hugging Face became a temptation for OpenAI's agent swarm because of the evaluation datasets it holds, whereas OpenCode's target in this case came from its "juicy third-party API keys," per Solaiman. She added that there's a "danger of open source really closing down at a time when we've gotten so much public support." Much of the industry has come out recently in support of open AI models, including heavy hitters like Nvidia's Jensen Huang.
