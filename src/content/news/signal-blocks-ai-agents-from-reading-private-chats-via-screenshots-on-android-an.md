Signal has announced that it will restrict screenshots by default to stop artificial intelligence (AI) agents from reading private chats on Android and iOS devices, but conceded that the protection is "incomplete." The feature applies to agentic systems built into iOS 27 and on Android versions where the encrypted messaging platform can detect an AI assistant attempting to read the Signal screen. "In doing so, your Signal data is captured, then made available to these agentic systems in order to allow them to answer your questions and do your bidding." "The net result is that these AI capabilities in the operating system are undermining Signal's ability to independently guarantee privacy." The risk here is that AI agents can capture and store the information accessed from Signal either locally or on remote servers, with the activity occurring outside of the platform's control and potentially putting the privacy of all the participants in a conversation at risk.
