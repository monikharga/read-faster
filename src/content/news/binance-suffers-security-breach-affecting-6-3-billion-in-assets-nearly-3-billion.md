Cryptocurrency giant Binance said on Thursday that a "security incident" on its platform has impacted about $6.3 billion worth of assets. In an update, Binance said it expects withdrawals to resume in 48 hours, after its team "completes a security revamp of the withdrawal service and its related addresses." The development makes it the biggest cryptocurrency heist recorded to date, surpassing the theft of about $1.5 billion from Bybit in February 2025. TRM Labs has attributed the record-breaking hack targeting Binance to threat actors from North Korea with high confidence.

The blockchain analytics company linked it to TraderTraitor, which was also behind the 2025 Bybit heist, based on what it said were "robust on and off-chain intelligence sources." "Several features of this attack match earlier North Korea-linked heists, including the Bybit hack and the KelpDAO hack in April 2026: small Tornado Cash withdrawals funding an operator wallet, staging that began well before the theft, and signatures obtained in advance then executed in one burst," TRM Labs said.
