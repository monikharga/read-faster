Rather than downloading another app, a growing number of agents can simply be texted like an ordinary person. Ask Proxy's biggest differentiator is that it "thinks like a woman," meaning it aims to anticipate your needs before you even ask, the company explains on its website. It works like a chatbot you can text like a friend, whether you need gift ideas, help planning an event, or recommendations for things to do that weekend.

Unlike a typical chatbot, Ask Proxy also takes a proactive approach. There's also a work-focused version, which the company explains "gets employees back to work faster, while keeping personal data off company systems." The personal version costs $1 per day. For businesses, pricing starts at $15 per employee per month for large companies and $23 per employee per month for small teams with fewer than 49 employees.
