Amazon has held talks about letting Meta's Muse AI agent shop on its online marketplace, according to a report from Fortune. The discussions, which could end Amazon's block on Muse, reportedly center on the kinds of products the AI agent recommends and the potential impact on the ecommerce giant's advertising revenue. As reported by Fortune, Amazon is concerned that it could lose advertising revenue by allowing Muse on its site, since it depends on actual humans seeing ads across its online marketplace.
