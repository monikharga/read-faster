The boss of ChatGPT-maker OpenAI says people should have more faith in AI firms to keep the tech safe, amid rising public concerns about the risks. "The world should trust that we are going to do the right thing because it's the right thing and we feel the magnitude of this," Sam Altman said on Tuesday during a conference in San Francisco. But critics say firms cannot be trusted to self-regulate, with left-wing Senator Bernie Sanders and former Trump adviser Steve Bannon among those calling for greater governance of AI.

Altman's comments came during an appearance at an annual conference hosted by software firm Salesforce. After Altman's comments, Mark Zuckerberg wrote on X, external every AI firm has the ability and the incentive "to take its own actions" towards safety.
