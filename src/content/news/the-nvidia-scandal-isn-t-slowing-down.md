For years, Jensen Huang has had an almost unprecedented power to shape AI's advancement as Nvidia's CEO, cofounder, and president. As The Verge wrote last week, Nvidia's chips are "the bedrock upon which advanced AI technology in the US is being built." Ever-increasing demand has consistently outpaced supply, leading AI CEOs to essentially bow at Huang's feet as they vie for more compute ahead of their much-hyped IPOs. Nvidia also has a varied investment portfolio of up-and-coming AI companies from neoclouds to infrastructure firms, worth nearly $100 billion, and Huang has President Donald Trump's ear.
