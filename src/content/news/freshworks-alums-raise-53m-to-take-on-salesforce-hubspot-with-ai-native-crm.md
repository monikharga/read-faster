Dreamteam co-founder and CEO Anand Venkatraman (pictured above, right) told TechCrunch that he had repeatedly encountered problems with traditional CRM platforms over more than 25 years in enterprise software, including at Salesforce and Freshworks. That experience led Venkatraman to co-found Dreamteam in 2025 with Senthil Kanthaswamy (pictured above, center), who spent more than nine years at Freshworks, most recently as a product leader at Freshservice, and Ramesh Parthasarathy (pictured above, left), who previously worked on AI and platform engineering at Freshworks. This acts as virtual memory, pulling together information from emails, meetings, and other customer interactions to build a shared record of customer relationships.

Four built-in AI agents use that information to research prospects, draft follow-up emails, flag potential risks to deals, and configure CRM workflows, with sales teams retaining control over what gets sent to customers. The startup has also replaced established CRM platforms at several businesses, including Salesforce at healthcare company DMS Health and HubSpot at other customers.
