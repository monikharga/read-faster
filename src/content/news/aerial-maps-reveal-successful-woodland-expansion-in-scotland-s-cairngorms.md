Editors have highlighted the following attributes while ensuring the content's credibility: A University of Cambridge–led team has used aerial surveys across a huge swath of the Cairngorms National Park in Scotland to create detailed 3D maps that show the beginnings of large-scale recovery of native woodland. The maps document a success story for the Cairngorms Connect nature restoration partnership: Young trees are spreading naturally into areas where woodlands have been missing for centuries, following a landscape-scale approach to deer management across the partnership's 60,000 hectares. The maps also show that habitat type matters for tree regeneration: Fewer new trees had established in boggy areas than in dry heathlands.

"The ability to map regenerating trees so precisely, at such a huge scale, is a breakthrough for monitoring nature restoration at a landscape scale.
