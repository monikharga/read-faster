Drone pilot Pete Short hunts for hotspots in the Cairngorms National Park which was devastated by a huge wildfire over the summer It is 06:00 and I'm meeting drone pilot Pete Short for a dawn patrol in the Cairngorms National Park. After a bumpy drive into the woodland, Pete launches a thermal drone into the early morning sky. "On the last thermal flight I did we found eight." "We might have something here," Pete says, as a white glow appears on the thermal image beneath a tree.

Three months after the fire first ignited, heat is still being detected beneath the ground. "It's really now cooking away," Pete says. "Without the thermal technology we would be totally blind to this." The wildfire began in the middle of July and burned across about 3,300 hectares – 33 sq km – of land, according to the Cairngorms National Park Authority.
