Editors have highlighted the following attributes while ensuring the content's credibility: The 67th Supplement to the American Ornithological Society's (AOS's) Check-list of North American Birds, which was published today in Ornithology, includes several significant updates to the classification of bird species found in North America, Central America and the Caribbean. Also of note, Momotus mexicanus (Russet-crowned Motmot) has been split into two species, giving Guatemala its first endemic bird species, M. American populations of two species, Ardea alba (Great Egret) and Numenius phaeopus (Whimbrel), have been split from their Old World counterparts, resulting in two new American endemics, A.

The Check-list, first published in 1886, is updated in annual supplements from the AOS's North American Classification Committee (NACC). The NACC reviews proposals submitted annually for taxonomic and distributional updates to the Check-list of North American Birds. This year, the committee revisited the case of Tringa semipalmata (Willet), an issue previously considered in 2017 (2017-A-10), based on a new proposal (2026-A-3), submitted by Pamela C.
