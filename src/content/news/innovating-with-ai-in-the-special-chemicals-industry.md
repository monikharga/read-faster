Connected data, AI, and advanced modeling are helping scientists accelerate research and develop more sustainable materials. For specialty chemicals manufacturer Lubrizol, digital transformation is all about giving researchers the tools to test ideas faster, make better-informed decisions, and develop more sustainable materials without compromising performance. Girish Rao, vice president of information technology at Lubrizol, says the company is building a digital foundation that connects business processes, modern data platforms, and cloud infrastructure to support its research and development (R&D) teams.

Data scientists can help identify patterns, test new hypotheses, and model possibilities before researchers conduct physical experiments. But, Rao emphasizes, "it's not about replacing scientific judgment; it is about giving our scientists better tools." Scientists, data scientists, and IT teams approach problems from different perspectives, prioritizing experimental validity, model performance, and platform security and scalability, respectively.
