The owner of high-end fashion brands Hobbs, Whistles and Phase Eight says it is investigating a cyber-security incident. According to TFG London - the UK arm of retail conglomerate The Foschini Group - it identified an incident on Sunday affecting some IT systems and "specifically impacting our Phase Eight, Hobbs, and Whistles brands". "We are actively investigating and working with external cyber security specialists," the company told BBC News in a statement.

The retail group reportedly told customers in an email that it did not believe customers' payment information had been affected by the incident. She told the BBC that while it was too soon to ascertain how the incident affecting TFG London's systems occurred, or whether it was related to other recent hacks, "the retail industry has some serious questions to answer about how exposed its systems have become".
