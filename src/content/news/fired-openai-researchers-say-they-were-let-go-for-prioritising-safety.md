Three former OpenAI researchers sacked for allegedly mishandling information claim they were instead let go because they raised safety concerns at the tech firm. "I believe we were fired for prioritising safety over the near-term interests of OpenAI as a corporation," Mikita Balesni posted on X, external alongside an open letter to the ChatGPT-maker, written with fellow ex-employees. The joint letter to OpenAI leadership was written by Balesni, Tomek Korbak and Jasmine Wang, who say they were all, until recently, safety or alignment employees at the company.

The former staff members raised concerns that their firings had made employees at the tech company "afraid to speak and operate in ways that, until last week, were an integral part of working at OpenAI". Reposting the letter, Korbak wrote, external: "For months, I'd been raising safety concerns that we're losing the ability to monitor what AI agents think, one of our best tools for catching when they misbehave.
