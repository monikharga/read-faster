The Seattle Times and Newsday are just the latest plaintiffs to take OpenAI to court, alleging copyright infringement. This is similar to lawsuits filed by The New York Times, Ziff Davis, Merriam-Webster, and Encyclopedia Britannica against OpenAI. The Seattle Times and Newsday also named Microsoft as a defendant in the suit, since Copilot is built on OpenAI's technology.
