A new study shows that many jurisdictions have fallen short of the foster care placement guidelines of the Indian Child Welfare Act of 1978. The law requires that when a state takes an American Indian or Alaska Native (AIAN) child into foster care, the state must first seek to place that child with a family member, a member of the child's tribal nation or another Native family.
