Scientists have learned a lot about what happens to the human body as we age. But there's still much to learn about how other species age, including dogs. Alexandra Genualdi, a student at Oklahoma State University who presented her research in September at the American Physiological Society's 2026 Comparative Physiology conference in Orlando, talks about her efforts to learn more.
