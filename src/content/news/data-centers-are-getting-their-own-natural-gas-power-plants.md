The numbers behind the off-grid gas power boom. Some developers aren't willing to wait and are instead opting to build their own off-grid, or behind-the-meter, gas power plants to fuel the AI boom. As of October 2026, data center developers have announced plans for more than 100 behind-the-meter gas power plants at facilities in the US, according to data from Cleanview, a market intelligence company.

But there's a growing trend of large data centers looking to build their own power supply, and this could shape the grid—and its climate impacts—for decades to come. When developers want to build a new industrial facility—like a data center—that requires a lot of electricity, they need to request a grid connection from a local utility or regional grid operator. That request kicks off a yearslong process in which grid planners study the existing grid to understand what would need to be built to safely deliver power to the facility without compromising reliability.
