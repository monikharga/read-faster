Join this expert-led webinar to learn how real-time identity context can help your SOC investigate threats faster, understand what's at risk, and respond with confidence. Identity and access management (IAM) teams know who has access to what. Security operations teams monitor suspicious activity and respond to threats.

But these teams often work with different tools, different data, and different priorities. An analyst might detect suspicious activity, but several questions remain: These answers matter. Traditional security monitoring was largely built around users, devices, and network activity.

Security teams also need to understand its permissions, connections, and potential impact. Instead of treating every alert as an isolated event, analysts can connect suspicious activity to the identity behind it, understand how far an attacker could move, and choose a response that limits damage without unnecessarily interrupting operations.
