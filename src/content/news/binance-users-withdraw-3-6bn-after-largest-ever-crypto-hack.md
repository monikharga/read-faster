Binance customers withdrew $3.6bn (£2.7bn) of assets from the world's biggest cryptocurrency exchange as it reopened on Sunday following what is believed to be the largest hack in history. Binance said on X: "Between 06:00 and 15:30 [GMT] on November 1, we processed $4.649 billion in withdrawals and $1.01 billion in deposits, resulting in a net outflow of approximately $3.64 billion." There was an "initial spike" in withdrawals, which later declined and "are stabilising as expected", it added. Binance also said withdrawals of more than two dozen major cryptocurrencies like Bitcoin and Ethereum have resumed and that more services will resume in phases once security checks are completed.

Funds linked to the Binance hack continue to be laundered, according to Alec Zebrick, senior manager in global services at Chainalysis, which has been working with the firm and governments to get the stolen money back.
