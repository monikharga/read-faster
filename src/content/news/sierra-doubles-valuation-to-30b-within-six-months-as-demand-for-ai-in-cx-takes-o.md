Enterprise customer experience company Sierra, co-founded by former Salesforce co-CEO Bret Taylor, said on Monday that it has raised $1.75 billion in a Series F round that doubles the company's valuation to $30 billion. The new raise comes within six months of its previous round, which valued the company at $15 billion. The new round was led by Greenoaks and Lightspeed Venture Partners, with participation from existing investors who have led previous rounds, including Benchmark, Sequoia, Greenoaks, GV, and Tiger Global.

That said, Sierra is working along similar lines as other customer experience players in the market, which is creating a platform to build agents and partner with model developers to provide choices to enterprises. As personal and work agents interact with enterprise systems, Sierra is also working on creating protocol definitions for agents with companies like Meta, Stripe, and Walmart. Voice AI company ElevenLabs is now valued at $22 billion; Decagon is valued at $4.5 billion; Wonderful is valued at $5 billion; and Parloa is valued at $3 billion.
