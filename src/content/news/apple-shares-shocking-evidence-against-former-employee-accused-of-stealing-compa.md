These new details emerged after the legal counsel for former Apple employee Chang Liu — who now works at OpenAI — handed over Liu's old Apple work laptop for investigation earlier this month. Apple now alleges that Liu used a confidential Apple circuit schematic in his work at OpenAI, as well as a tool that shares a name with an internal Apple engineering application. The company claims that OpenAI was "well-aware" of Liu's access to Apple data, and that Liu enlisted OpenAI colleague Yu-Ting Peng to help destroy evidence in June when he learned that Apple was investigating him.

According to Apple's initial filing, more than 400 former Apple employees now work at OpenAI.
