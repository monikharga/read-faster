"These tools execute complete attack chains autonomously at machine speed, generate and refine large numbers of tailored attack scripts in rapid succession, and adapt their payloads based on target responses," CERT-In said. The update follows an October 2026 alert issued by CERT-In, which warned of multiple cyber attack campaigns targeting non-banking financial companies (NBFCs), digital lending platforms, fintech entities, payment service providers, and cryptocurrency wallet operators. CERT-In also warned that attackers are targeting cloud infrastructure to execute commands and deploy malicious payloads, as well as publicly-accessible non-production environments to obtain shared credentials, tokens, or network paths, and then use them to pivot to production systems.

Other notable post-exploitation actions undertaken by the adversary are as follows - The development comes as CERT-In has published the Artificial Intelligence Security Auditing Guidelines, laying out specific requirements to ensure the "governance, security, privacy, fairness, transparency, resilience and auditability of AI systems." The framework calls for testing an AI agent's documented identities, authorized objectives, approved tool use, permission boundaries, and its adherence to the principle of least privilege (PoLP).
