25% off tickets now Back by popular demand: Save up to $300 on Disrupt Three hikers were rescued from California's Mount Shasta this week after using Google's AI chatbot Gemini to plan their expedition, according to the Chicago Tribune. A report from the Siskiyou County sheriff's office said that three young men set off on their hike at 3am and — although hikers are told to turn around if they haven't reached the summit by noon — they made it to the top at 7pm. The trio then tried to descend in the dark, called the sheriff's office to ask for directions, and spent the night in Mud Creek Canyon before being rescued the next morning by Forest Service rangers and volunteers.
