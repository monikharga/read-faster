AI provider keys that customers stored on Railway were stolen in an October breach and used to commit an estimated $740,000 in unauthorized spending, the platform-as-a-service provider said yesterday. The company builds and runs apps for its customers and stores their secrets, such as API keys and database credentials, as environment variables. The attacker decrypted variables in at most 1.85% of workspaces, about 100,000, and its database queries looked for AI provider keys.

In an October 26 update, the company told customers asking about refunds for unauthorized AI spend that "Railway is unable to refund customers for spend performed with compromised API keys." It encouraged them to contact the AI providers directly and offered to escalate their requests. According to the company's incident report, the decrypted data also included linked-account tokens for 1,852 users: BYOK agent keys and GitHub OAuth tokens. Refund terms differ by AI provider, according to the company's incident report: Customers are asked to report charges they did not make through a charge report form.
