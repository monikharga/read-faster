There has been near universal praise from critics, with review aggregator Metacritic calculating its average score at 99 out of 100 based on 150 reviews., external Nintendo's The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time has held the top spot since 1998 - but Metacritic confirmed to the BBC that this has now officially been overtaken. Some schools have reminded parents and pupils to prioritise study over the game, which has an 18 rating, external for "strong graphic violence, strong sexual themes and use of illegal drugs". The game, set in the fictional US state of Leonida, follows two protagonists, Lucia and Jason, through the seedy underbelly of America after an "easy score goes wrong".

Even from the set-up and credits, it's feeling pretty good," Aleana Fernando, who lives in Auckland, New Zealand, told BBC Newsbeat soon after she started playing. Once the game was released at midnight UK time, gamers were equally enthralled by the game's graphics and attention to detail.
