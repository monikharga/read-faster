Blockchain analysis firm Chainalysis has revealed that its ongoing investigation into the agentic cyber attack targeting its systems also exposed names, usernames, password hashes and live tokens for all users. "At this time we continue to have no evidence that any investigations data has been accessed or Chainalysis attribution data has been manipulated." Chainalysis said it has implemented additional proactive monitoring systems to detect unusual activity, adding it took the Know Your Transaction (KYT) user interface and API offline, alongside other address screening products and the login page. "We still encourage you, where necessary, to reset your password on other systems where you may be reusing credentials." The development comes less than a week after Chainalysis said it was the target of a cybersecurity incident in which an external actor leveraged offensive agentic techniques to obtain unauthorized access to its systems and internal tools.
