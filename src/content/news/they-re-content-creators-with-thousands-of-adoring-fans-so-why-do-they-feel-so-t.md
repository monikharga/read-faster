(We used pseudonyms to protect the privacy of the people we interviewed.) "The thing that I got right, that I loved for the longest time and now has almost become the bane of my existence, is creating relatable comics," he told us. "Now, I'm stuck … I would rather die than make relatable comics, but I've built an empire of relatable comics. Content creation is, according to some estimates, a $300 billion industry.

Becoming a content creator has officially elbowed out old standbys like doctor and astronaut as the job that Gen Z and Gen Alpha most aspire to have. Countless online courses now promise to teach the ropes of content creation, while Arizona State University will be offering a new content creation major.
