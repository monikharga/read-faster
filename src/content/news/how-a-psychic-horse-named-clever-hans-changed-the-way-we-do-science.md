At the turn of the 20th century, many great scientists, psychologists and philosophers became fascinated by the possibility of "psychic phenomena" like telepathy, clairvoyance or ghosts. Broad, attended séances and thought about the possibility of precognition—the ability to foresee future events. However, some of these "psychic researchers" went even further and began wondering about the possibility of telepathy in animals.

Schiller engaged in heated discussions about the psychic abilities of animals. His article on "psychical research" for the Encyclopedia Britannica has a section about "equine mathematicians"—horses with supposedly paranormal mathematical abilities that they demonstrated by tapping out solutions to equations with their hooves. In 1914, Schiller wrote an article for the SPR about Rolf, "the thinking dog of Mannheim" (in southwest Germany).
