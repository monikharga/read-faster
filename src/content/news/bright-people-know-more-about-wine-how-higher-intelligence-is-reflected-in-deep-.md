But what happens in domains where incentives such as good grades or financial rewards don't apply and where socioeconomic status is not a significant factor? Intelligence researcher Maximilian Krolo of Saarland University has investigated whether intelligence can predict the acquisition of deep knowledge in a specialized domain. The findings have now been published in the journal Intelligence & Cognitive Abilities in an article co-authored with Professor of Psychology Timothy C.
