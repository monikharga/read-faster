Greek Prime Minister Kyriakos Mitsotakis came to San Francisco on Monday night to promote Greece, but he also did something that heads of state on trade missions don't typically do. The prime minister, speaking with this editor at an event hosted by Endeavor Greece, the local arm of a global nonprofit that supports entrepreneurs in emerging and growth markets, described his Bay Area trip as partly a fact-finding mission. But the trip was also meant to build bridges; indeed, he was pitching anyone in tech who might consider setting up shop in Greece, which is set to regain developed market status next year from MSCI, the influential index provider.

"I think it's another indication that the economy is doing well and that Greece is no longer treated as a special case," Mitsotakis said. Mitsotakis was just as eager to talk about how Greece has spent its money.
