When we think about Europe's winter weather, the Indian Ocean is probably not the first place we look. In my research, however, I look at how these familiar weather systems fit into a much larger climate system. My colleagues and I asked whether an ocean thousands of kilometers away could leave a detectable signature on Europe's early winter.
