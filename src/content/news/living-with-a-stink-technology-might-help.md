The operators of Newby Island landfill have been ordered to take action over odours Milpitas, an affluent city in California's Silicon Valley tech hub, has an odour problem. "When I first moved here, I could not open my window for over 10 years," says Chia-Ling Kong, an engineer for a tech firm who lives around half a mile from the dump. While things have got better in recent years, Kong says she and her neighbours must still contend with around "two or three hours of bad smells a day".

The operators of Newby Island landfill have been ordered by local authorities to use drones and install monitors to systematically track and measure the chemicals responsible for bad odours. Just like in Milpitas, where Kong is part of a citizen's campaign against the odours, communities around the world are raising a stink about bad smells making day-to-day life miserable. Odour consultant Micheal Bull says people are increasingly concerned about neighbourhood stinks.
