As demand grows for real-world data to train robots, India's Humyn Labs has raised $47 million in Series A financing to help U.S. Humyn Labs has developed its own two-camera hardware to record people performing everyday tasks. Agarwal, the former CEO of Indian gaming company Nazara Technologies, founded Humyn Labs in February with Ishank Gupta (pictured above, left), a former executive at the brewing giant AB InBev.

KGeN has a network of contributors across emerging markets, and that network helps Humyn Labs collect data in people's homes, the founders told TechCrunch. He added that Humyn Labs also uses software to track hand movements, interactions with objects, and other details, turning raw recordings into data that AI models can learn from. Humyn Labs says it has 12 paying customers, including frontier AI labs, robotics companies, and developers of world models.
