Editors have highlighted the following attributes while ensuring the content's credibility: A new study has developed a method to estimate rubber yields across Hainan Island, one of China's main natural rubber–producing regions. Using high-resolution remote sensing data, meteorological data and a limited set of field measurements, the team mapped annual dry rubber yields from 2021 to 2025. Accurate yield estimation can help stabilize global supply chains and inform field management and industrial policies.

Existing methods for rubber yield estimation are broadly classified into statistical models and physical models. Statistical models can be accurate, but they require extensive field data for training and offer limited insight into the biological processes that determine yield. Physical models such as CASA can represent photosynthesis and other ecological processes, but they rely on fixed empirical parameters and estimate potential yields under ideal conditions.

To bridge the gap between potential and actual yield, the researchers developed a multistage framework that couples the CASA model, SOLARFLUX, a conversion module and a parameter calibration scheme.
