The new feature, called "Visuals," allows users to turn Suno songs into AI-generated video clips that automatically lipsync to lyrics, or make images via prompt descriptions that can be used as thumbnails and album artwork. The exact layout differs across Suno's desktop and mobile interfaces, but users will find a new Visuals tab under Create mode on each version. Web users can also access Visuals by selecting the three dot menu on a song in their library.
