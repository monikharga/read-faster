Apple on Monday introduced new controls that allow Mac users to give more granular access to data they give to apps, including those that use artificial intelligence (AI) agents. The changes, currently previewed in macOS 27.3 beta 2, relate to increased transparency and more options for two of its APIs: Device Control and Data Access, and Full Disk Access. In addition, apps that use Full Disk Access for disk backup or the new Accessibility settings for assistive technologies can request entitlements to offer the same experience for new installations that they offered before macOS 27.3.

"Additional entitlement details will be available soon." The development comes a little over a month after Apple began taking steps to tighten controls around a macOS setting called Full Disk Access (FDA) due to security risks posed by artificial intelligence (AI) agents.
