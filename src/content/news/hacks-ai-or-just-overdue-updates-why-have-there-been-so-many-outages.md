In the past week, there has been a massive Microsoft outage, an apparent tech glitch which grounded all US flights for hours, and issues for the National Security Agency (NSA). These are all separate incidents - but the fact they have all come so close to each other has got people wondering if they might be linked. Problems with the NSA's internal communication system and, separately, Apple's iCloud and Apple Pay were also blamed on things going wrong during system updates.

But some recent problems are in fact related to cyber-security vulnerabilities. "I would be very surprised if all of these cyber-attacks and the attackers behind them were not using AI to accelerate themselves," Helme told BBC Radio 4's Today programme. Since the summer, AI companies such as Anthropic and OpenAI have decided it's too risky to give the general public access to the most advanced models before allowing certain companies and institutions to use them.
