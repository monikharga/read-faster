Whether it's scrolling through endless choice, spotting fake reviews, or working out if a "deal" is a deal at all, Black Friday sums up a lot of online woes. Retailers promise bargains - but the sheer number of offers can leave us feeling no closer to finding the best deal. But are they any good at finding Black Friday deals?

Crucially, 36% of respondents who said they would be shopping around Black Friday didn't plan to use an AI tool at all. Amazon launched Alexa for Shopping for UK users in November and OpenAI launched a Shopping Research tool on ChatGPT days before last year's Black Friday. We asked four models to find a 55-inch TV deal, using the free versions of many of the most popular tools.
