Big, noisy and coming to a town near you - why the data centre backlash is growing Until a few years ago many of us may have been blissfully unaware of what a data centre was. It's putting opponents of data centre expansion on a collision course with US President Donald Trump, who has dismissed the pushback as a "sick conspiracy", even as public anger grows. Here's what you need to know about the data centre boom, and why opposition is growing.

There are about 12,250 data centres around the world, according to Data Center Map, external, a company that tracks both operational and pre-operational sites. Data centres have typically been located near to cities where it's easier to access high voltage power lines or water resources for cooling.
