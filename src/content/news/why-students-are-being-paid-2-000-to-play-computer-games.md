Roehampton University's Overwatch Team winning Team of the Year 2022 As parents across the country prepare to wave their children off to university, conversations about family finances and the cost of living are growing ever more frequent - and fervent. So roll up the next generation of gamers, who this year can apply for as esports scholarship from the University of Roehampton - with more than 100 handed out so far. It is different from standard video gaming as esports includes spectators, much like traditional sports.

Roehampton is offering esports scholarships to undergraduate and postgraduate students who show that they can balance academic progress and video-gaming excellence. "This scholarship is about recognising talent in a field that has a growing popularity both around the world and among our students," University of Roehampton registrar Mark Ellul said. Former esports scholar Cameron Dray found out about Roehampton through the esports scholarship.
