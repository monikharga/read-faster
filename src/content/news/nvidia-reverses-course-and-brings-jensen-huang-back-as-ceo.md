government doesn't have any evidence that Huang engaged in any illegal activity. A long list of high-profile tech executives were pushing the board — in social media posts and publicly aired interviews — to bring Huang back, including SpaceX and Tesla CEO Elon Musk, Replit CEO Amjad Masad, Epic Games CEO Tim Sweeney, Atoms founder Travis Kalanick, and Y Combinator CEO Garry Tan — all of whom signed a petition pushing for reinstatement.
