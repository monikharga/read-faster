A single attacker using AI agents pulled emergency dispatch records covering seven Taipei districts, researchers at Acronis's Threat Research Unit said last week. The agents also found a Taipei fire department platform exposing patient data from 2018, including ECG readings and ambulance video footage, without a login. He aimed the agents at hundreds of organizations in at least 11 countries, including emergency services, banks, universities and military systems.

The most serious confirmed harm there involved emergency-services data. The agents also went after five other targets tied to Taiwan's emergency services: The report does not say whether the affected organizations or Taiwan's authorities were told, or whether the exposed data can still be reached. Outside emergency services, the agents pulled data from an account table at a Taiwanese lending firm through SQL injection.
