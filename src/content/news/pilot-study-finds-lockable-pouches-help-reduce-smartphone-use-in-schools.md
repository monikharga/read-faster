Editors have highlighted the following attributes while ensuring the content's credibility: Smartphone use during the school day dropped significantly across eight Northern Ireland secondary schools. The introduction of lockable smartphone pouches in secondary schools in Northern Ireland has reduced smartphone use in schools, according to research by King's College London. This evaluation was commissioned to begin filling that gap." "Given the evidence of the wide-ranging benefits to schools, the Department should consider strengthening its current guidance restricting the use of smartphones in schools.

Schools should be allowed to choose from an approved range of strategies that best fit their setting." The evaluation used a mixed-methods design across nine Northern Ireland secondary schools, though only eight engaged fully. Researchers tracked behaviors and attitudes before the pouches were introduced and at follow-up points through the year, using online questionnaires on homework, classroom disruption, PE and extracurricular participation, peer interaction, smartphone use, and measures of anxiety, depression, and smartphone and social media addiction.
