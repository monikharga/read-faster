Nvidia is in talks to guarantee billions in debt financing for AI cloud provider Nscale's planned Anthropic data center in West Virginia, The Information reported Thursday. Anthropic has signed a $44.6 billion contract for the project's first building alone, according to Nscale's IPO filing. The proposed arrangement would help Nscale secure financing for the electricity and construction costs of its West Virginia campus.

Under what's known as a residual value guarantee, Nvidia would step in if Nscale defaulted on construction payments, temporarily take over the facility, and help find a buyer and new tenant, according to The Information. Anthropic's contract with Nscale covers the first 460-megawatt building at Nscale's West Virginia campus, which is expected to use Nvidia's next-generation Vera Rubin chips. That includes $1 billion in convertible financing committed to Nscale in September.
