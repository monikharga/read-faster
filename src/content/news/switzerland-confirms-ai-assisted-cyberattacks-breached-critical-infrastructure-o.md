Attackers using AI agents have broken into computer systems at Swiss organizations in four of five confirmed attacks, the first such cases in the country, the country's cybersecurity agency said yesterday. The agency's first notice, a security advisory published on Tuesday, said the agents had compromised the victims' IT systems in every case known at that point. The attacks involve what the Swiss agency calls agentic offensive frameworks, AI agents that find security weaknesses and carry out attacks on their own.

Its advisory named ARTEX and CyberStrikeAI as examples of agents reachable over the internet, not as the tools used in the five cases. The agents are often used to scan for known, unpatched weaknesses and can run many attacks in parallel, the agency says. Raphael Reischuk, co-founder of Switzerland's National Test Institute for Cybersecurity, expects a larger wave of attacks and says companies underestimate the risk.
