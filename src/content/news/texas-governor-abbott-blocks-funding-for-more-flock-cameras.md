As backlash grows over Flock's AI surveillance cameras, Texas Governor Greg Abbott has frozen state spending on them. The move came just ahead of the publication of a Texas Tribune investigation that revealed the state spent over $30 million on Flock cameras. Flock cameras have increasingly drawn ire from across the political spectrum as they've become the subject of privacy concerns.
