In December 2025, the European Commission said the platform "deceives users" by allowing people to pay for a blue verified check mark, because it is not "meaningfully verifying" who is behind the account. The US Department of Justice (DOJ) has filed an application, external with the EU's General Court in support of Musk's and X's attempts to dismiss ​the case. It claims that under a section of law in the Statute of the Court of Justice of the EU, a state may intervene in disputes before the court if it "can establish an interest in the result of the case to the court".

The DOJ said the US "has a clear interest" in ensuring any Commission decision was applied consistently with "how territorial jurisdiction is generally understood in international law". "The European Commission's fine isn't just an attack on X, it's an attack on all American tech platforms and the American people by foreign governments," Rubio wrote in a post on X.
