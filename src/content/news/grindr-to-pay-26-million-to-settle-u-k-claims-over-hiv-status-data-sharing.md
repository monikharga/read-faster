Online dating app Grindr has opted to pay £26 million ($35.1 million) to settle a lawsuit in the U.K. users regarding that pre-2020 period." The company also said it has since revamped its privacy program as of 2020 and emphasized that the platform "remains a safe space for users," while committing to transparency, user control, and responsible data practices. It all goes back to April 2018, when a Norwegian non-profit research group SINTEF uncovered that Grindr was sharing users' HIV status and last tested date with two companies, Apptimize and Localytics, enlisted to optimize its apps.

"Grindr has never sold, nor will we ever sell, personal user information – especially information regarding HIV status or last test date – to third parties or advertisers," Grindr insisted at the time.
