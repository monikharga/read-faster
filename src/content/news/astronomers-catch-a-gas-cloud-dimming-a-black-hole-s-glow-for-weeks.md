Some active galactic nuclei (AGNs)—galaxies that are powered by actively feeding black holes—undergo rapid changes in their appearance, which manifest as a change in their spectral properties. These are called "changing-look" AGNs. Their appearance changes over time, getting brighter or dimmer, or losing or gaining certain spectral features.
