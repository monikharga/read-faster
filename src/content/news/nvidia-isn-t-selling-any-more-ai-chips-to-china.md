Nvidia has stopped sending its advanced AI chips to China, despite the approval it received from the White House to do so in December last year. The announcement was made by interim Nvidia CEO Colette Kress during an earnings call on Tuesday, marking a notable shift for the company that, under former CEO Jensen Huang, fought for the right to ship its powerful Hopper architecture H200 chips to China. "Our guidance ⁠does not have any licensed Hopper data center products included, and we are no longer shipping ​licenced Hopper data center products to China," Kress said.
