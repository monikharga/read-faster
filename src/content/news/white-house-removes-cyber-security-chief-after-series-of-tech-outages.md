Sean Cairncross (left) was appointed by the Trump administration The top cyber-security official in the US government is leaving his post after a little over a year in the job, US media report. CBS, the BBC's US news partner, and other media report that White House National Cyber Director Sean Cairncross has been fired after losing the confidence of White House chief of staff Stephen Miller. There has been no official confirmation of the removal of Cairncross but Secretary of State Marco Rubio posted on X, external to say Department of Defense chief information officer Kirsten Davies was the new national cyber director.

Cairncross was previously a lawyer in the Republican Party, before becoming the chief operating officer of the Republican National Committee. In the first Trump administration, he was part of the team of the White House chief of staff.
