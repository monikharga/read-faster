The biggest deal yet was the $105 billion backstop for OpenAI's PORTS-Pike data center campus in Ohio, announced in August. In exchange for that loan guarantee, OpenAI promised that the campus would exclusively use Nvidia's equipment, the company said. She reportedly reduced the backstop guarantee for the OpenAI project from $250 billion down to $105 billion.

Under Kress, Nvidia clarified in its quarterly CFO commentary that guaranteeing a loan doesn't mean the company is providing the financing itself. The guarantee only takes effect once the customer has secured the financing needed to build the facility and install Nvidia's products. So if all of that begins and unfortunately they are delayed or they have a challenge in terms of building that, our guarantee doesn't exist anymore," Kress said to analysts on Tuesday during the quarterly conference call.
