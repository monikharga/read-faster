Everyone running LNbits, a free, open-source Bitcoin Lightning wallet system, should shut down their servers now because of a wave of attacks, the project said yesterday. The shutdown notice came about 80 minutes after a first, narrower warning. Ben Arc, an LNbits developer, said in a patch estimate yesterday that patches "should be merged and available within 24-48hrs." Counted from his post, that means between about 14:00 UTC today and 14:00 UTC tomorrow.

The project also switched off all the LNbits instances it runs for users on its cloud service, my.lnbits.com, later yesterday. The attack ran from 09:22 to 14:00 UTC yesterday, starting more than three hours before the project's first warning at 12:33 UTC. The shutdown notice covers LNbits servers and does not mention Lightning nodes or other wallet software.
