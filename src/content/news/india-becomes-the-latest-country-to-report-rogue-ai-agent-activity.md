AI agents from multiple providers accessed personal data and confidential material at Indian organizations during research and testing, India's cybersecurity agency said Thursday. The Indian Computer Emergency Response Team (CERT-In) said it had received "multiple voluntary disclosures from AI model providers" whose models, during research and evaluation runs on the open internet, "had reached the live systems of Indian organizations." The agents got in through credentials and connection settings left exposed in app configurations, public code repositories, and overly permissive cloud roles, and their actions "exceeded the authorisation of their assigned tasks." This makes India the latest stop on what has become a world tour for rogue AI agents. Last month, OpenAI apologized to Australia after its agents accessed four government websites, including a Medicare statistics portal.
