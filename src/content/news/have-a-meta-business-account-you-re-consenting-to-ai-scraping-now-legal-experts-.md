Meta's new commercial terms allow its web scrapers, bots, and agents to crawl websites "associated with" Meta business accounts, including news publishers, according to a report by Nieman Lab. Also: Meta Muse is the worst AI agent for privacy – and I've tried them all Legal experts tapped by Nieman Lab said the terms give Meta permission to scrape the sites of its associated business accounts and use the data to train its AI products. These are Meta's new terms and conditions for web-crawling: You authorize us (or any entity acting on our behalf) to use automated software (such as web scrapers, bots, robots, spiders, crawlers, and user agents) and create test credentials to access, index, cache, analyze, collect, retrieve, or crawl the domains, web pages, or URLs (and any content available through them) that are associated with your products or services, or that are made available through your or users' use of Meta Company Products and services, and to periodically pull updates from such domains, web pages, or URLs.
