Zach Yadegari has enjoyed an immense amount of success for someone who only recently graduated from high school. Founder of the AI-fueled calorie tracking app Cal AI, Yadegari is only 19 but his company has rocketed to viral fame over the past two years. Discussing his social strategy onstage, Yadegari noted that an early sign of success came when an influencer's post about them led to approximately a thousand new users over the course of two days.

I guess linear in the sense that every post they did would get a certain number of viewers that would convert." The Forbes 30 Under 30 alumnus told the audience that part of his company's strategy was to negotiate rates with influencers, promising them certain payouts for a certain scale of reach. A lot of influencers are "broke college kids" who "just want cash," Yadegari added.
