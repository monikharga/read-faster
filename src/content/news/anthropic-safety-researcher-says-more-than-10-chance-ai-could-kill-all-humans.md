Anthropic makes the AI tool Claude, which is used as a chatbot and as a tool to help coding A top safety researcher at Anthropic's has warned AI is advancing so quickly there is a greater than 10% chance it "could kill all humans" within the next decade. It comes after the Financial Times reported, external Anthropic withheld its latest model from the AI Safety Institute, which tests AI. Leading figures in the AI field have been raising the alarm about the safety threat the tech poses for years, with the heads of OpenAI, Google Deepmind and Anthropic saying as much in 2023.

OpenAI, Anthropic and Meta all disclosed hacks carried out by their AI tools. Major figures in the space have been calling for AI development to be slowed in recent months, including Anthropic bosses Dario Amodei and Jared Kaplan.
