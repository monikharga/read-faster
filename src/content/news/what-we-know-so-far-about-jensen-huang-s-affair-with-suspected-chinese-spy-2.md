Watch: Who is suspected Chinese spy at centre of Nvidia affair investigation? US intelligence officials believed that Chinese businesswoman Alice Huang, who had a close relationship with Nvidia CEO and co-founder Jensen Huang, was a spy for Beijing, according to a New York Times report. Jensen Huang (left) and Alice Huang - also known as Huang Le - pictured at an event US officials believed that Alice Huang had been recruited to spy by China's top intelligence agency, the New York Times reported, external.

Alice Huang, who is of no relation to Jensen Huang, was a top executive at Singapore-based data centre firm Megaspeed between March and November 2023. Surveillance reportedly revealed communications between Alice Huang and an American citizen, who was subsequently identified as Jensen Huang. It remains unclear what, if any, intelligence Alice Huang – who is also known as Huang Le – allegedly gathered and passed to Beijing.
