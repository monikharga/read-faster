The sell-off, which started in the US and then spread to Asia and Europe, has seen trillions of dollars wiped off the stock value of major tech firms. The share falls also come on top of concerns about whether the huge amounts being spent on AI will pay off. The sell-off in US stocks meant the so-called magnificent seven tech companies, including Apple, Nvidia and Tesla, shed a trillion dollars in value in a single trading day, with Nvidia shares sliding more than 9%.

Shares in Elon Musk's companies SpaceX and Tesla also plunged, falling 8% and nearly 11% respectively and shaving more than $300bn off the firms' market value. The index is now in what market watchers call correction territory, which is where an index or company value falls at least 10% below its recent high.
