New data from hiring systems firm Greenhouse found that "nine in ten US employers (90%) believe they've turned away real candidates because those applications seemed fake or AI-generated." Greenhouse clarified that it doesn't know whether employers are putting applications through AI detection tools or not. "As jobseekers rely more on AI to polish and send out their job applications, employers are finding it harder to tell who's actually right for the role," the report stated, echoing an ongoing push-and-pull between applicants and hiring teams. The report shows how a tough economy and an influx of AI hiring tools are colliding, arguably to benefit employers over applicants.

Greenhouse noted that, ironically, AI tools are helping hiring managers while also making resumes untrustworthy, and applicants would rather be interviewed by AI than not at all.
