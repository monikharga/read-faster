During the incident, Storm-0940 used ARTEX, a Go-based agentic AI penetration testing framework that was made closed-source in the wake of its abuse in cyber attacks targeting South Korean financial institutions, to speed up scanning and reconnaissance, password spraying, and OAuth application abuse at downstream victims. "Storm-0940's use of ARTEX accelerated the speed of compromised application abuse to authenticate to target environments through applications that had already been granted access, allowing the threat actor to operate without requiring an interactive user sign-in," Microsoft said. "AI-accelerated follow-on activity included reconnaissance, application credential changes, and attempts to modify privileges that could enable expanded or persistent access." Storm-0940 was first detailed by Microsoft in November 2024, revealing that the threat actor has been leveraging a botnet called Quad7 (aka CovertNetwork-1658) to conduct highly evasive password spray attacks.

"Between October 10 and 14, 2026, Storm-0940 infrastructure at 144.172.64[.]99 conducted an automated cross-tenant password-spray campaign targeting 327 accounts across eight organizations to gain access to Microsoft 365 and Exchange Online," Microsoft said.
