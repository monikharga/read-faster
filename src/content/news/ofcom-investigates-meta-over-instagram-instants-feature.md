Ofcom is investigating whether Meta breached its duties under the UK's online safety laws with the launch of its "Instants" feature on Instagram. Under the UK's sweeping online safety rules, platforms including Meta must carry out and update risk assessments to comply with a range of duties, including to tackle illegal content. "Our online safety laws are clear – significant changes to platforms must be risk assessed before they're launched," said Ofcom enforcement director George Lusty.

"You wouldn't launch a new car without testing it properly, and it's the same for online services." What the Online Safety Act is - and how to keep children safe online Instants rolled out to the app's users in May, external as a way for people to share spontaneous images - something that was once what the app was all about.
