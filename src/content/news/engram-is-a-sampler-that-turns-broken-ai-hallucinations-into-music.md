It's a sampler and groovebox that uses AI to mangle incoming audio and even hallucinate completely new sounds. This isn't a "push-button, get-song" device, aimed at creating something that sounds ready for top-40 radio. It's about creating experimental, uncanny sounds and pushing AI audio models beyond their limits.
