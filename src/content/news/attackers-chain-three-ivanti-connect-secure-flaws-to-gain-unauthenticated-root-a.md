It's worth noting that the security bugs are part of an exploit chain that was first documented by Volexity earlier this month as weaponized in conjunction with an authentication bypass flaw (CVE-2026-104935, CVSS score: 8.6) by a previously undocumented threat actor dubbed UTA0576. CVE-2026-104935 was added to the KEV catalog on October 13, 2026. Patches for both CVE-2026-111357 and CVE-2026-118527 are now available in the following versions of ICS - "At the time of discovery of exploitation, Ivanti had a fix developed for CVE-2026-104935 which was already in QA for supported versions, and we made the decision to not delay getting a fix to customers that resolved the primary exploitation path when the LPE was discovered," Ivanti explained.

Federal Civilian Executive Branch (FCEB) agencies are required to apply the fixes for CVE-2026-111357 by November 2, 2026, and CVE-2026-118527 by November 13, 2026.
