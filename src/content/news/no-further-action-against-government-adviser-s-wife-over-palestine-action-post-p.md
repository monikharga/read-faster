Police say no further action will be taken against the wife of the government's national security adviser after she posted on social media referencing Palestine Action. Sarah Helm, who is married to Jonathan Powell, wrote on X on Thursday: "God help us authors who support Palestine and Palestine Action." She was responding to a discussion about the author Sally Rooney, whose novels will not be supplied to booksellers in the UK following her announcement that she intends to use her royalties to support the proscribed organisation. "No further police action will be taken," the force added.

On Friday, the Met Police had said it was "in the process of being assessed to determine whether any further police investigation may be required" after it was "made aware of an X post on Thursday, 15 October referencing Palestine Action".
