In dozens of local ballot measures, voters rejected data centers. Sean kicked it off by noting that Elon Musk "seems to have basically nothing out of donating half a billion dollars" to Republicans, while his companies are now headquartered "in a state that has, remarkably, turned about as blue as it could possibly turn." And Kirsten said that while data centers probably weren't the main cause of the "blue wave," they've become an "an increasingly grassroots issue, in part because of how hyperscalers and companies have gone about" getting their projects approved. Sean O'Kane: First off, let's talk about the fact that one of the biggest data center builders was also the largest donor to the Republican Party.
