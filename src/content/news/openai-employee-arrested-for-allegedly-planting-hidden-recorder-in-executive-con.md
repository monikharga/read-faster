"Abdulrahman Alfozan allegedly concealed a covert recording device inside his employer's executive conference room to secretly capture sensitive communications about the company's super intelligence technology," Kash Patel, Director of the U.S. "Protecting American SI [super intelligence] innovation is essential to safeguarding our nation's economic strength and national security, and the FBI views that mission as one of our highest counterintelligence priorities." According to the complaint, on or around September 30, 2026, OpenAI personnel discovered a surreptitious audio recording device inserted into a USB port on the back of a television in an executive conference room. Closed-circuit television (CCTV) footage obtained from within OpenAI's office showed Alfozan appearing near the executive conference room on multiple dates prior to the discovery despite having no authority to access or participate in meetings taking place in the executive conference room.
