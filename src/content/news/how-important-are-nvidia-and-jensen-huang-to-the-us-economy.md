No company or chief executive has arguably been more consequential to the development of AI in recent years, with the US microchip manufacturer under Huang positioning itself as a critical cog in the development of the world-changing technology. While Nvidia is not as much of a household name as other Big Tech players such as Apple, Amazon and Microsoft, the company has been just as integral to the booming US stock market of recent years. The billions of dollars being funnelled into AI and the construction of data centres has bolstered the American economy, with Nvidia being one of the biggest spenders.

That was because it was unclear whether Nvidia would change strategy and potential rein in spending - a move could have had wider implications for the US economy, but which looks unlikely now Huang has returned. The fears were sparked due to Nvidia being a key source of finance for many firms, as well being a microchip supplier.
