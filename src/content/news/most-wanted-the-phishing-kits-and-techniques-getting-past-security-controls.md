Every phishing attack has to get past email security, web filtering, and safe browsing before it reaches an employee. At Push Security, we measure this as breakthrough rate: The share of attacks that get past email security, web gateways, and safe browsing to reach the user. Every browser-based attack starts with a lure, and each control in its path sees only part of the attack: When the page loads in the user's browser and the user interacts with it, that's a breakthrough.

A kit with a high breakthrough rate is one that upstream controls are missing. A low count in email or proxy logs doesn't prove that a kit is rare, because the kit may be getting past those controls without a trace. From August to October 2026, one kit ranked first by breakthrough rate every month.

DOMDIFFER, an AiTM phishing kit that targets corporate Google and Microsoft accounts, broke through 2-4x as often as the next kit each month.
