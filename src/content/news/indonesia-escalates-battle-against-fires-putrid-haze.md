Editors have highlighted the following attributes while ensuring the content's credibility: Indonesia has intensified efforts to fight wildfires with cloud seeding and water bombing as thousands of people nationwide battle to breathe through smoke and haze, officials said Friday. More than 5 million people on the islands of Borneo and Sumatra were directly exposed to the risks of a putrid haze from dozens of fires across the vast archipelago, according to Deputy Health Minister Dante Saksono Harbuwono. "Vulnerable groups that must be evaluated are children, the elderly, pregnant women, people with asthma and outdoor workers," Dante said.

"My children often have coughs and colds, maybe because there is a lot of smoke." Dante said prolonged exposure to toxic air can cause serious long-term health complications. He said the ministry has distributed more than 5 million masks across Indonesia—a country of 289 million people dealing with the effects of a supercharged El Niño weather event dubbed "Godzilla." Disaster agency BNPB said Friday that the government has intensified efforts to battle wildfires, with some success.
