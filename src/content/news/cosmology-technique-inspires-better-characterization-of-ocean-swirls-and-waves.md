Editors have highlighted the following attributes while ensuring the content's credibility: A new study from Caltech uses a mathematical framework borrowed from cosmology to analyze ocean data and characterize individual flow features in the churning chaos.

With the technique, the researchers are able to measure and characterize features in maps of the ocean surface, allowing them to differentiate ocean flows that otherwise look identical when evaluated with standard analysis tools. The work is important for sharpening our understanding of ocean physics and for improving ocean and climate models, which must capture not just how energetic the ocean's motions are but also their structure. The ocean is incredibly complex.

Such processes occur constantly in oceans all across the planet, and understanding them and other ocean features is important for accurately modeling the ocean and predicting how it impacts our climate.
