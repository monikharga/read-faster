Products such as Microsoft Teams and Outlook have been out of service for much of Friday Microsoft has said its services have been fully restored after a worldwide outage affected several of its tools, causing disruption. The outage hit more than 70 countries, disrupting everything from business operations to government services and personal users. Users began reporting they were having trouble accessing Microsoft 365 products on Thursday.

By Friday, the far-reaching issue was affecting institutions from the Japanese government and the Philippines Supreme Court to the Australian spy agency, and trains, hospitals and courts in North America. Services were beginning to recover by Friday afternoon UK time, nearly 24 hours after the issues first began to emerge.
