Knowing this, NASA is interested in understanding the effects that exposure to these rays has on humans, particularly long-term exposure for astronauts who travel to deep space, the moon and, eventually, on longer missions to Mars. "We know cognitive changes from exposure to radiation in space are a real risk," said O'Banion, a member of the Del Monte Institute for Neuroscience, whose previous research showed that exposure to cosmic radiation could accelerate the onset of Alzheimer's disease. Knowing which mechanisms are impacted and how may help the development of countermeasures to protect or lessen an astronaut's exposure to space radiation." Most recently, his lab demonstrated that radiation-induced changes in the brain's immune system differ between male and female brains.

URochester researchers carried out the experiments at the NASA Space Radiation Laboratory at Brookhaven National Laboratory on Long Island using simulated space radiation produced by particle accelerators. Neuroscience graduate student Mark Osabutey, MBChB, led the project that used single-cell RNA sequencing to examine nearly 16,000 microglia, or brain immune cells, from male and female mice exposed to simulated galactic cosmic radiation.
