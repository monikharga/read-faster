Cryptocurrency giant Binance said on Thursday that $6.3bn (£4.7bn) of digital assets have been impacted by what is thought to be the biggest crypto hack on record. Two analytics firms have attributed the incident, which eclipses a record-setting $1.5bn hack on the Bybit exchange in 2025, to a North Korean hacking unit called TraderTraitor. Check Point's chief information security officer Jayant Dave said the losses estimated by Binance made it "the largest crypto theft on record".

AI-powered investigations company TRM Labs said in a report that several elements of the Binance attack resemble earlier North Korean-linked incidents, including the Bybit hack. Chainalysis, a crypto analytics firm, also said it has "high confidence" that the North Korean group are linked to the hack.
