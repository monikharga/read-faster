China's economic growth picked up in the three months to the end of September, rebounding from a three-year low as the artificial intelligence (AI) boom helped boost exports. China's National Bureau of Statistics said on Monday that the economy "sustained steady development against pressure" and showed "momentum with new growth drivers and improving structure". With exports climbing, China's trade surplus - the amount of goods and services it sells abroad compared to what it buys from other countries - is on track to overtake the record $1.2tn (£900bn) seen last year.

Oxford Economics researcher Sheana Yue said manufacturing and high-tech exports have boosted China's growth, despite weak investment and domestic spending. 'Investing in people': Can China's new push to boost spending revive the economy?
