AI leaders like Dario Amodei and Sam Altman have expressed their fears that AI is developing too quickly, but if AI models become capable of "recursive self-improvement," they'll be able to advance on their own without human intervention, rendering any efforts to "pace the frontier" futile. I'm not sure how to visually represent the idea of an AI building itself, but you could really lean into the "self-improvement" part and forget the "recursive" aspect — it's a last-minute Halloween costume. Carry around a self-help or productivity book like "Atomic Habits" or anything by Malcolm Gladwell, a yoga mat, and a huge bottle of water.

You are recursive self-improvement. (While we're talking about her, you're only allowed to do the black turtleneck Holmes costume if you're a natural blonde, or if you have a blonde wig.) What you're going to do is make yourself look big and fluffy. Add a distinctive hat, glasses, or other accessory to make yourself look more like a ChatGPT dot.
