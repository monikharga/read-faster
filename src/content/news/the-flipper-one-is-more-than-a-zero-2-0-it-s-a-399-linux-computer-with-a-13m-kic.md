Tech > Services & Software > Operating Systems > Linux Backers are going big on the $759 Ultimate Bundle, but Flipper warns that rising RAM prices could affect production. The Flipper One crowdfunding campaign has broken the $13-million mark, smashing the funding raised for previous Flipper devices and demonstrating strong interest in the successor to the Flipper Zero. The $399 Flipper One is a pocket Linux computer focused on networking, with delivery estimated for July 2027.

If the Flipper Zero, with its NFC, RFID, sub-GHz, and IR, was kindergarten, the Flipper One is a college degree: a pocket Linux computer focused on networking. If that $399 feels like a lot, it's worth keeping in mind that, according to Flipper, the price only covers production costs and that the RAM and UFS storage account for at least half of the total hardware cost.
