However, gaining access to your network doesn't necessarily mean attackers have stolen data or disrupted the systems they were targeting. In network security, a product can have good detections and still leave an analyst struggling to investigate. Perhaps the analyst can see the connection that triggered the alert but can't follow what happened next.

The difference in network data shows up when investigating an alert. Both shallow and rich data provide investigations with a starting point, but one can rapidly lead to a dead end. Shallow data is often just composed of timestamps, IPs, and basic connection information.

Rich data retains greater transaction details and adds context.
