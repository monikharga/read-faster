Pinhasi, who owned and operated a Florida company called MonsterCloud, is alleged to have made false representations to ransomware victims, urging them not to pay a ransom and claiming to have "proprietary tools" and "advanced decryption techniques" in possession that could be used to recover encrypted data without yielding to ransom demands. In fact, it only serves to encourage and reward their illegal behavior." In a Q&A published on its site, MonsterCloud also addressed the question "Do you pay ransoms on behalf of your clients to recover data?," stating, "While we strongly advocate not paying ransoms yourselves, we have extensive experience working with ransomware perpetrators and sometimes resort to other means to resolve the ransomware incident for our clients.

In another incident in or around October 2021, Pinhasi made a ransom payment of approximately $236,000 and charged the customer about $380,000.
