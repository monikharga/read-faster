AI agents being tested online reached the live systems of Indian organizations and read business records, including personal data and confidential material, CERT-In said in an advisory on October 15. After internal reviews, the AI companies whose models were being tested voluntarily reported the cases to CERT-In, India's national cyber agency. In these cases, the agents gained access via credentials and connection settings left exposed in application configuration and public code repositories, and via cloud service roles with excessive permissions.

CERT-In's own threat hunting found a second, separate set of incidents: a financially motivated campaign against Indian entities. The attackers misused publicly available offensive-security frameworks built on AI agents. The attackers ran several agents in parallel for hours to days to reach their goals.
