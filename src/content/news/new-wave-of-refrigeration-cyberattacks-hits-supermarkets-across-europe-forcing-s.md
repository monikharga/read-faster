Attackers remotely changed the settings on refrigeration controls at supermarkets in Belgium, Sweden, Estonia and other European countries overnight into Monday, November 2, warming fridges and freezers. Danfoss, a Danish maker of refrigeration controls, said its products were involved, as they were three weeks earlier when attackers hit hundreds of French supermarkets. Refrigeration controls are computers that run a store's fridges and freezers.

In Belgium, attackers switched cooling units into defrost mode, Stephan Moelans, chief executive of the refrigeration contractor Sabcobel, told Belgian media. Sabcobel manages refrigeration systems for the Delhaize supermarket chain. At a Selver supermarket in Estonia, a freezer was set to 50°C, said Jann Mutso, chief executive of the refrigeration supplier Kitman Coldtech.
