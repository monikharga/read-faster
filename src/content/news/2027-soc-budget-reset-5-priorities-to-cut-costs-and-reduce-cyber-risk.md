According to ANY.RUN's estimates, SOC teams could recover 450 analyst hours per 1,000 investigated alerts through faster threat analysis and IOC research. As 2027 approaches, here are five priorities to reduce SOC costs, recover capacity, and strengthen protection against costly cyberattacks. For 2027, the focus should be on reducing investigation costs, improving analyst productivity, and avoiding unnecessary escalations.

ANY.RUN reports up to 20% lower Tier 1 workload and 30% fewer Tier 1-to-Tier 2 escalations, helping SOCs handle growing workloads with less pressure to expand their teams. ANY.RUN estimates that faster behavioral analysis through its Interactive Sandbox, combined with less manual threat research, can save 450 analyst hours per 1,000 investigated alerts. Consider a SOC handling 4,000 alerts annually: Illustrative calculation based on ANY.RUN's estimate and an assumed hourly rate.

The budget priority: Compare projected alert volumes, investigation workloads, and staffing requirements before approving additional headcount.
