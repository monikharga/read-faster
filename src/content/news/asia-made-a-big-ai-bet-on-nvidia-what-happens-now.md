Jensen Huang's extraordinary return as Nvidia's boss rocked not only its shares in New York but also sent shockwaves through the artificial intelligence (AI) industry around the world. Tech-heavy stock indexes in Japan, South Korea and Taiwan went on a rollercoaster ride this week. The chipmaker is a "symbolic bellwether" of the AI trade that is fast becoming key to places like South Korea, Taiwan, Singapore and Malaysia," says Dylan Loh, an international studies lecturer at Nanyang Technological University.

That means Taiwan is vulnerable to changes in AI tech demand, says Paul Cavey, founder of Taipei-based research firm East Asia Econ. Huang said earlier this year that Nvidia plans to invest around $150bn (£114.4bn) a year in Taiwan at a launch to celebrate the firm's planned headquarters on the island. To a lesser extent, South Korea and Japan are also exposed to the industry, Cavey adds.
