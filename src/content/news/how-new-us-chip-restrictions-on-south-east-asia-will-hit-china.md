The move not only restricts the export of advanced chips, but also access to their computing power, which Chinese firms currently rent abroad to train artificial intelligence (AI) models. South East Asian countries in particular have invested heavily in data centres to power their AI ambitions - and Chinese firms seeking computing power overseas have been a key market. Some of the firms behind them are accused of helping China evade US export controls on advanced chips.

One of those is Singaporean data centre firm Megaspeed, whose former director Alice Huang is suspected of being a Chinese spy. Megaspeed, the biggest buyer of Nvidia's chips in South East Asia, is accused of illegally smuggling chips to Chinese companies after purchasing them for use in data centres in Malaysia, Indonesia and Thailand.
