How close is Donald Trump to Jensen Huang? Jensen Huang wasn't just another tech billionaire seeking the president's ear. Washington has restricted exports of its most advanced processors to China since 2022, believing they could help Beijing develop its military and technological capabilities.

Trump appeared sympathetic to this view, allowing Nvidia to resume sales of certain chips to China last year in return for a share of the revenue, although its most advanced Blackwell chips remain off limits. The allegations surrounding Jensen Huang's reported affair with Alice Huang, of no relation but a former executive at Singapore-based company Megaspeed, have raised the possibility that a foreign intelligence operation was targeting not just a powerful businessman, but someone with unusually close access to the American president. US intelligence reportedly suspects Alice Huang of having links to Chinese intelligence.

It was also being pressed to act on concerns about Megaspeed, an Nvidia customer suspected of helping divert advanced chips to China.
