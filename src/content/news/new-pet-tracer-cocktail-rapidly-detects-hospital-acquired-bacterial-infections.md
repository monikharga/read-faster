A novel radiotracer cocktail can accurately identify multiple strains of Pseudomonas aeruginosa—a leading cause of hospital-acquired infections—within hours, according to new research published in The Journal of Nuclear Medicine. By targeting multiple strains of the bacteria, this rapid detection approach has the potential to enable targeted therapy and reduce the use of broad-spectrum antibiotics among critically ill patients.
