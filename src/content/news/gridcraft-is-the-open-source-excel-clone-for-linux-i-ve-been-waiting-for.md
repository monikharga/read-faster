Tech > Services & Software > Open Source If spreadsheets are your thing, but Microsoft Office isn't, you might want to give the open-source clone, GridCraft, a try. ArtCraft, the development team behind PhotoCraft (and a number of other similar apps) reverse-engineered yet another de facto standard app: GridCraft, meant to mimic Microsoft Excel. ArtCraft's collection is an important step forward for open source and the Linux operating system because some of the apps they've built have long been considered roadblocks to user adoption of Linux.

Time will tell, but these apps seem like a big step forward for Linux and open-source to me. Also: I tried the free Adobe Premiere clone on Linux – and DaVinci Resolve has competition Although the developers say that these apps are 1:1 replacements for the original apps, that's not 100% accurate, and my experience with GridCraft confirms that. Like the other ArtCraft apps, GridCraft runs natively on whatever OS you are using (Linux, FreeBSD, MacOS, or Windows).
