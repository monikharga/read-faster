Most people know by now that Google Wallet can do things besides store your credit cards, but you might have missed that Google has added a lot to its digital wallet app in just the past few months. Also: I tried all 5 Googlebooks, and they feel like a whole new kind of laptop I've started using Google Wallet more and more recently, and I'm consistently surprised when I find new ways to avoid reaching for my real wallet. Also: How I forced Google Search to give me the results I want and can trust again – it's easy You will need to have package tracking enabled in Gmail, but once you have that on, you can see your detailed receipts, tracking numbers, and shipping status inside the Wallet app.

In June of this year, Google announced a partnership with the Transportation Security Administration that made Wallet the first digital wallet compatible with the PreCheck Touchless ID program.
