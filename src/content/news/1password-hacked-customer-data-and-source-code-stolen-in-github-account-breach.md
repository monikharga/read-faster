Popular password management software 1Password on Wednesday disclosed a security incident involving its backend systems that led to the unauthorized access of some customer information, but emphasized that customer password vaults and other secrets were not accessed. "While we took immediate steps to contain the incident, the attacker gained access to some of our backend systems and accessed a limited amount of customer account information from a subset of our customers, including names and email addresses," the Toronto-based company said. It noted that no customer vault content, including secrets, passwords, and other vault items, account recovery keys, or the keys that secure them, was accessed or compromised as a result of the breach.
