When Keelan Miskell began winding down his crypto startup this year, he didn't think its data was worth much: the users were anonymous, the company just a few years old. Now tech undertakers are selling the data remains to AI labs. Its Asset Hub platform helps founders sell their code and workspace data—emails, chats, documents.

CEO Dori Yona has seen these transactions, once rare and "somewhat taboo," become increasingly routine, with 47% of SimpleClosure deals now including code or data sales. Founder and CEO Brendan Mahony says Sunset values a dying client's assets by head count, years in operation, volume of data, and how the team used its tools. Particle board, he says, is a good analogy: "This operational exhaust data is like the wood shavings that everyone thought had no value, but it actually has a ton." Demand for defunct companies' data emerged suddenly last year, as AI training labs hit the limits of public and synthetic datasets.
