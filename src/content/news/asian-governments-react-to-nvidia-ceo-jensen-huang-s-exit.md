On Sunday, November 8, Nvidia's board launched an independent investigation after reports that Huang was targeted by a Chinese spy operation; Huang admitted to an affair with Alice Huang, a former executive at Singapore-based chip company Megaspeed. China's foreign ministry rejected allegations on November 10 that a Chinese businesswoman linked to former Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang was a spy, calling them groundless and a smear against Beijing. On Friday, a spokesperson reiterated opposition to export controls that disrupt global supply chains and stated the ministry was not aware of reports linking restricted Nvidia chips to a Shanghai data center associated with Alice Huang.

In Singapore, authorities seized the passport of Alice Huang, former CEO of Singapore-based cloud computing company Megaspeed, in June 2025 as part of an investigation into possible false declarations regarding server purchases, according to the Ministry of Home Affairs. The Singapore ministry said police took her passport to ensure she remained available for questioning, as she was not based in Singapore.
