After making laptops and screens ubiquitous in schools across the country, Norway is bringing back textbooks in a bid to reverse a sharp decline in pupils' reading and writing skills. "There is reason to believe that the introduction of screens in schools happened too quickly, before we had sufficient knowledge about their effects and about what constitutes good pedagogical use of digital devices," Norway's Digitalization Minister Torgeir Micaelsen told AFP. Starting in 2023, primary schools in Oslo were even supposed to equip every pupil with a digital device.

Ironically, Oslo municipality has just dropped its requirement for one device per pupil in primary schools, giving schools more leeway in choosing their teaching tools. It's the way it's used, when the tool becomes more important than the content," primary school principal Silje Ween told AFP.
