Take, for instance, the developer of Omarchy, who claims: Omarchy is leaning fully into the future and the age of agents. That's right, if you're an Omarchy fan, you'd better get used to AI, because it's officially a part of the system. Also: I struggled with Hyprland for months, until this Arch-based distro fixed it Now, before you get too up in arms, it's not like it's embedded into every corner of the OS, but it is certainly there.

If you glance at the official Omarchy manual, you'll catch this passage: Omarchy treats AI coding agents as first-class citizens, but it doesn't pick a favorite for you. Not only are the AI agents geared toward coding, but the desktop environment is Hyprland, which is a puzzle in and of itself. Don't get me wrong, I really like Hyprland, but unless you want to memorize a ton of keyboard key combinations, you're going to have a rough time with this desktop.
