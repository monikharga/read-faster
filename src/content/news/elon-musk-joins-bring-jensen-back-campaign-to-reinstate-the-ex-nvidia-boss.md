SpaceX boss Elon Musk and Nvidia's former chief executive Jensen Huang Allies of Jensen Huang are trying to rally support for the former Nvidia boss to get his job back after resigning last week over a scandal involving extramarital affairs, one involving an alleged Chinese spy. SpaceX boss Elon Musk and Tim Sweeney, chief executive of Fortnite-maker Epic Games, are among those backing a campaign to get the AI chip giant's board to "Bring Jensen Back". An open letter calling for Nvidia's board to give Huang his job back while an internal probe is conducted has received more than 175,000 signatures, including Musk and Sweeney, according a website named "Stand with Jensen".

"We write with gratitude for Jensen Huang and with a clear demand to the board: bring Jensen back while an independent investigation establishes the facts, unless and until you can provide compelling evidence that his removal is in NVIDIA's best interests," the letter said.
