Editors have highlighted the following attributes while ensuring the content's credibility: Gratitude lists can help anyone recognize the good things in their lives and feel more positive. Research by BYU psychology professor Jenae Nelson suggests that becoming a grateful person takes more than counting blessings. In a study of more than 1,000 adults in the United States, Nelson and her team found that traditional gratitude lists can help people feel grateful.

But expressing gratitude to God or to specific people can also help cultivate empathy and inspire a desire to do good for others. "It's relatively easy to feel grateful, especially when you do the typical practices like counting your blessings or keeping a gratitude list. "It's really good to be able to notice the good things in your life, but it's better to notice the people in your life," explained Nelson.
