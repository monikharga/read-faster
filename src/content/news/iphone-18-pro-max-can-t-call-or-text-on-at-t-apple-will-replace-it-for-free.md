Tech > Services & Software > Operating Systems > Mobile OS > iOS Some iPhone 18 Pro Max phones on AT&T are stuck in SOS mode. If your iPhone 18 Pro Max has lost service on AT&T and is unable to make calls, send texts, or even use mobile data, two new software updates won't be enough to get it working again. Also: iOS 27.0.1: Apple fixes annoying iPhone 18 Pro restart issues Apple has now confirmed to ZDNET that an issue affecting "a small number of iPhone 18 Pro Max users" on AT&T's network may cause their devices to lose service.

"We released iOS 27.0.1 earlier this week and strongly encourage all iPhone 18 Pro Max users to update now, and are also issuing a carrier settings update today," Apple said. "Update to iOS 27.0.1 immediately — Settings > General > Software Update." Apple has not provided an exact number of affected devices, though it has only observed the issue among iPhone 18 Pro Max users on AT&T.
