A federal judge denied a motion by xAI seeking to dismiss an NAACP lawsuit accusing the SpaceX-owned company of failing to obtain permits needed to operate the gas turbines powering its Colossus data center outside Memphis. Suing on behalf of the NAACP, the Southern Environmental Law Center said the AI company had "installed and operated at least 35 combustion turbines and other sources of air pollution at the Colossus site without ever obtaining the necessary preconstruction or operating air permits." It also accused xAI of violating the Clean Air Act. In a statement, the NAACP's director of environmental and climate justice Abre' Connor described this as "another important victory in our effort to hold xAI accountable." (A similar motion to dismiss from the Department of Justice was denied earlier this month) "The courts have repeatedly rejected attempts by xAI and the DOJ to dismiss the NAACP's Clean Air Act case, affirming our right to pursue justice for communities impacted by pollution," Connor said.
