The emergence of powerful vibe-coding agents and tools like Reverse Engineer Anything has caused chaos in the music gear world, leaving companies scrambling to figure out how to respond. In the last several weeks, as it's become clear that no piece of music gear is safe from Claude and a subreddit full of curious tinkerers, companies have begun making more and more of their devices open source. The biggest name to decide "if you can't beat 'em, join 'em" is inMusic, the parent company of Akai, Moog, Native Instruments, and more.
