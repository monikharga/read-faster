Anthropic just went public with a major IPO this week An Anthropic employee and mathematician claims to have used the company's artificial intelligence (AI) chatbot to prove the famed Riemann hypothesis. Levent Alpöge said, external on Friday, a day after Anthropic made its US stock market debut, that over the course of about six weeks, he used the Claude chatbot to create a detailed proof of the maths problem. As for the Riemann hypothesis proof put forward on Friday, it was found using a non-public version of Anthropic's Claude.

It is a nearly 170-year-old maths problem that deals with prime numbers and is widely considered to be the single most famous problem in maths, in large part due to how complex it is. The complete textual proof that Alpöge put forward in Lean, a computer language used for confirming maths proofs, is over 250,000 pages long.
