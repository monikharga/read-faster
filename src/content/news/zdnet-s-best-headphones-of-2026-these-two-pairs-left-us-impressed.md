Headphones with a unique and useful feature, like the Beats 360's purpose-changing earpads, also catch our attention. Also: ZDNET's best products of 2026: Expert picks for phones, laptops, and more We also like headphones that embrace the latest wireless and audio technologies, such as LE Audio and Auracast, which is exactly what the Sony WF-1000XM6 does. I've had a chance to go hands-on with dozens of headphones this year, but these two stand out for their combination of features, audio quality, and value relative to price.

The Beats 360 earned their spot as ZDNET's pick for best headphones of the year, thanks to their unique take on earpad modularity, innovative audio engineering, sweat- and water-resistance, and native compatibility with Apple and Android. Beats created a pair of modular headphones that change functionality when you switch their earpads: the Performance Knit cushions are sweat-wicking and suited for exercise, while the UltraPlush cushions provide a comfortable, casual, and immersive listening experience. The Beats 360 include the Performance Knit cushions with purchase, and a pair of UltraPlush cushions is $50.
