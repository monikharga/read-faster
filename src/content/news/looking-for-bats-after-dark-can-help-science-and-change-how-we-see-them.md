These recordings also help us identify which bats are present, as different species produce calls that vary in their frequency, shape and timing. Bats are sometimes described as "microbats" and "megabats". These aren't formal scientific groupings, but the terms are still commonly used to distinguish smaller bats from larger species.

In Australia, smaller bats are mostly insect-eaters while our larger flying foxes mainly feed on fruit, nectar and pollen. Bats are captivating creatures. Bats also play a vital role in ecosystems.

Flying foxes pollinate native plants and disperse seeds, while insect-eating bats help keep insect populations under control. Their nocturnal habits mean most people rarely encounter them up close, while misinformation and negative perceptions about bats persist.
