An iOS sandbox escape published on GitHub lets an app read other apps' files on iPhones running iOS 26 and 27. Apple's updates for iOS 26 and 27, released yesterday, do not list a fix. The exchange told users to delete apps installed from untrusted sources, such as sideloaded or manually signed apps, and to update iOS and all their apps.

No one involved has said whether an app from the App Store could use worse_query. Of these sources, only Erosion's developers list iOS 27.2.1, which came out yesterday, and none lists iOS 26.7.3. Yesterday's updates, iOS 27.2.1 and iOS 26.7.3, fixed two iMessage flaws and several Bluetooth bugs.

The security notes for iOS 27.2.1 do not mention worse_query or any sandbox issues, nor do the iOS 26.7.3 notes.
