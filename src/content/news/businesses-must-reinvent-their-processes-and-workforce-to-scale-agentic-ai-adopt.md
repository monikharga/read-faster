Also: Business adoption of AI agents tripled this year - as measurable ROI emerges The question most business leaders struggle with is determining the viability of their business model and the impact of AI agents on existing processes and desired outcomes. Nearly two-thirds of business leaders are reevaluating their business models due to advances in agentic AI. The other key challenge business leaders face with AI agents is that technological change is outpacing their organizations' ability to adapt; this will continue to challenge businesses for the foreseeable future.

Process and workflow transformation is key to scaling AI agents, and most business leaders are not ready. By 2030, 74% of business leaders noted that nearly half of business processes will be redesigned or rebuilt around AI agents.
