There was no alternative to developing AI, Anthropic boss Dario Amodei said The head of AI company Anthropic has called for the pace of development of artificial intelligence models to slow down and to be closely monitored. The bosses of two rival AI firms, Sam Altman of OpenAI and Elon Musk, have both said they agree with Amodei. In Amodei's essay, called We Must Pace the Frontier and published on Saturday, he proposed a three-point plan that included independent monitoring of AI models as they are developed, industry-wide regulation and global regulation.

"I agree with Dario that we need to pace the frontier," wrote OpenAI CEO Sam Altman on X. 'There is a possibility of human extinction,' former Anthropic employee says about AI Altman sounded similar safety concerns in a new interview, telling Fortune magazine that standards were "not at a place" to push AI capabilities much further.
