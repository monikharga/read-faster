Around the world, a love/hate attitude toward AI is very much the vibe right now. Around the world, a love/hate attitude toward AI is very much the vibe right now. More than half of people worldwide say that AI products and services make them nervous, according to a report from Stanford University.

In a Gallup poll in May, 71% of US adults said they would oppose construction of a new AI data center in their area, far more than the 53% who said they would oppose a new nuclear power plant. According to Pew, half of US adults now say they use a chatbot, more than twice the number who said they did in 2023. And it's a global habit: More than a third of adults across all 38 OECD countries (a group of the world's wealthiest democracies) report having used generative AI tools in the last three months.
