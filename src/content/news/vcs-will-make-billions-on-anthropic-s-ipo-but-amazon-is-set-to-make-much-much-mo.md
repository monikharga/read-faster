At its $965 billion private valuation, a 1% stake is worth nearly $10 billion, so, for example, Menlo's roughly $14 billion position is only about 1.5% of the company, according to recently reported Bloomberg figures. While the venture firms may lack sizable percentage stakes, Anthropic sold large stakes to strategic partners, companies that are also its business partners. Amazon first backed Anthropic in 2023 with a commitment to invest up to $4 billion in the AI company.

Instead of taking stock upfront, Amazon structured much of its investment as convertible notes, a type of debt that converts into shares upon an IPO or sale. According to the S-1, Amazon holds convertible notes with $6.2 billion in principal outstanding as of June 30. But as Anthropic's valuation soared across successive funding rounds, the shares those notes will convert into became far more valuable.

Amazon's notes could convert into as many as 1.84 billion shares, according to the S-1.
