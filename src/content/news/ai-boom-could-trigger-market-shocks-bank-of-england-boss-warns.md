Watch: Governor of the Bank of England Andrew Bailey says quality of deepfakes 'alarming' Artificial intelligence (AI) could trigger financial market shocks and the UK needs to be prepared for them, the governor of the Bank of England has warned. Andrew Bailey said the central bank is watching the huge amounts of money being invested in AI "very carefully" and cautioned that "not everybody always wins". And two of the largest AI companies in the world – Anthropic and OpenAI – are also preparing to sell shares in their firms on the US stock market in moves which many believe will lead to hundreds of billions of dollars more flowing into the industry.

"There is a large, very large, amount of investment going into this sector now, and of course that's natural because it's a major area of growth," said Bailey.
