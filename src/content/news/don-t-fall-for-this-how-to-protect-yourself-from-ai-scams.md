Cloned or new websites that look polished and professional in rapid time and at a low cost Romance scams in which AI is used to remember victims' personal details and keep conversations going over weeks or months AI-generated deepfake videos and voice-cloned audio is used to impersonate family members or close friends asking for money in an "emergency" Error-free, personalised phishing emails, text messages and social media posts can be generated by AI on a huge scale Some of the old scam-avoidance techniques may now be less successful due to the benefits of AI.
