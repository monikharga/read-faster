Watch: What we know about the Microsoft global outage overnight A major global Microsoft outage has caused problems, or in some cases disabled entire services, including emails and artificial intelligence products. Microsoft has said the outage was not caused by a cyber-security incident, but efforts to fix the issues have so far been largely unsuccessful. Schools, GPs and businesses hit by global Microsoft outage as company scrambles to solve issue Microsoft 365 Services, including Teams, Outlook and Microsoft's artificial intelligence software Copilot, have been affected by "connectivity problems".

More than 430 million people use Microsoft 365 apps according to Microsoft's website. The full scale of the outage has not been confirmed, but monitoring website Downdetector had 140,000 people from 74 countries report issues with Outlook on Thursday evening. Microsoft 365 is also used by companies worldwide and is expected to have a knock-on effect to other services.
