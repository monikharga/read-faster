On Monday, President Trump praised Nvidia's decision on Monday to reinstate founder Jensen Huang as the company's CEO and president, saying on his social network Truth Social that "the United States Government has no reason to believe that Jensen's return to NVIDIA poses any risk to National Security." The Nvidia board's statement had also referred to consultations with "senior levels of the United States government" as informing its decision. There's also indication that President Trump may have been aware of the decision before the announcement by the Nvidia board. Speaking to a PBS journalist over the weekend, he said of Huang "he's back where he should be and we see he did absolutely nothing wrong." Asked to clarify about whether he meant Huang would be reinstated, Trump said, "I hope so, I hope so." Huang was accused of having multiple extramarital relationships, including one with a direct report at Nvidia and another unnamed employee.

Huang was an executive at a Singapore-based company called Megaspeed, which had purchased billions in Nvidia chips over the past couple of years.
