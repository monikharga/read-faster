That can mean scheduling an appointment, organizing a calendar, researching a trip, sending an email, making a reservation, shopping online, or reminding you about something days later. Instead of worrying about all the details getting buried, Caddy connects to your calendars and conversations, then identifies things that may require action. Comma is designed to manage tasks across both work and everyday life.

Comma carries tasks through to completion, checking its own work and determining when a task is finished. It can work across a user's browser, computer, and files, while remaining accessible remotely through messaging apps such as Signal, Telegram, and WeChat. Comma maintains context and memory across devices and conversations, and can deliver daily briefings, flag urgent matters, and summarize recorded calls.
