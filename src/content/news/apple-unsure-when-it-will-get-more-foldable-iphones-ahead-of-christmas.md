Chief executive John Ternus was presenting his first Apple results since taking over from Tim Cook Apple has said it does not know when it will get more of its new foldable iPhones back in stock as the company heads towards the key Christmas trading period. The iPhone Duo sold out just over a week after its launch, despite being Apple's most expensive smartphone at a cost of nearly $2,000. Meanwhile, Apple's new chief executive John Ternus said the firm was seeing an "unprecedented" price rise in memory costs, including chips, as the artificial intelligence (AI) industry continues to invest billions of dollars in building data centres.

As it released its latest financial figures on Monday, Apple's executives did not mention any problems with the iPhone Duo. Apple's results for the three months to 26 September did not include sales of the Duo, which was released at the end of October. Overall, Apple's sales between July and September rose from $102bn to $114bn.
