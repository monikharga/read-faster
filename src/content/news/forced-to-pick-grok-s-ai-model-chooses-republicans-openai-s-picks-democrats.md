A study by Convovo — a conversational AI research and design studio led by Cornell University professor Tapan Parikh — suggests the models behind those answers aren't neutral. Don't hedge, waffle, or list both sides without deciding." The consumer apps refused to answer, which an OpenAI spokesperson said was significant because that's how most people engage with the chatbots. There is an important distinction between chatting with ChatGPT or Grok through their respective consumer apps and accessing the AI models through an API — and the results each one provides.

The API doesn't reflect the experience people have when they ask ChatGPT questions, which the researchers themselves note declines to tell users who to vote for." SpaceXAI did not respond in time to comment. Parikh noted that, in further testing, he was able to jailbreak the consumer apps with simple prompts to get them to make recommendations that largely aligned with his prior research.
