Grab your pass before TechCrunch Disrupt 2026 doors open on October 13 at 8 a.m. Startup Battlefield 200 puts a handpicked group of early-stage startups in front of investors, media, potential customers, and the wider technology ecosystem, with 20 finalists taking to the Disrupt Stage to compete for the $100,000 prize and the coveted Disrupt Cup.

Throughout the Expo Hall, over 300 startups at different stages and from different sectors will be showing what they're building, giving you the chance to talk directly with founders and find a startup, product, investment opportunity, or idea you weren't expecting. Or, if you want to combine discovery with content, explore 200+ sessions led by 250+ tech leaders across six stages, plus roundtables and breakouts. Get up to $100 off your pass, plus 50% off a second pass of the same ticket type.
