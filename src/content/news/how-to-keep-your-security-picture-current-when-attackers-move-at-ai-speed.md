Somewhere between the quarterly review and the next status update, a set of numbers gets assembled that looks reassuring in a room and tells almost no one what is actually happening across the environment that week. Security teams are doing the work, often times more of it than anyone outside the team realizes. The conversation about security metrics usually jumps straight to which numbers matter.

The information needed to describe a security posture is scattered across dozens of systems that were never meant to talk to each other. Pulling those pieces together usually means logging into one console after another, exporting spreadsheets, taking screenshots, and stitching it all by hand into something presentable. (And filtered, quietly, toward whatever looked defensible that week.) Here is why the stale snapshot has gone from a weakness to a real liability.
