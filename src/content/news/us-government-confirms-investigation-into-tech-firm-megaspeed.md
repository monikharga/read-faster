The United States has confirmed it is investigating computing company Megaspeed, suspecting it of violating US export controls by helping to ship tech giant Nvidia's chips to China. In a joint statement, the US Department of Justice and Department of Commerce said they had been investigating Singapore-based firm Megaspeed since 2024. Concerns emerged in recent weeks after Nvidia founder Jensen Huang revealed he had an extramarital affair, allegedly with former Megaspeed executive Alice Huang.

Megaspeed is the biggest buyer of Nvidia chips in South East Asia and is suspected of helping divert advanced chips to China. Alice Huang and Jensen Huang pictured together in Taiwan in June 2024 Following this enforcement action, the Department of Justice took the lead role in the criminal investigation of Megaspeed. The Nvidia scandal has created tensions between the US Treasury and Commerce departments over shipments of high-end chips to Megaspeed.
