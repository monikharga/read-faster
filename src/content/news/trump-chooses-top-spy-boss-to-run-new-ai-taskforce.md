US President Donald Trump has named the director of national intelligence as the head of a new taskforce on AI which, he said, will coordinate government engagement with consumers, companies and "religious organizations". Trump - who is unilaterally attempting to rebrand AI as "super intelligence" - wrote on social media: "The Super Intelligence Force will coordinate the Federal Government's engagement with Consumers, Public Interest Groups, Religious Organizations, Critical Infrastructure Providers, and Super Intelligence Companies." As well as Clayton, a former chair of the Securities and Exchange Commission, Andrew Ferguson, head of the Federal Trade Commission, will be on the taskforce along with Emil Michael, undersecretary of defense for research and engineering. He said the US is leading the race ahead of China, adding: "And, frankly, I want to keep it that way because whoever wins AI, wins." President Xi Jinping recently travelled to Washington for a state visit.
