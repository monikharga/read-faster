One user said Apple had helped him to replace his faulty iPhone Duo Apple has acknowledged some of its iPhone Duo handsets have developed problems with their screens, just days after the highly-anticipated smartphone went on sale. The Duo's launch, which attracted crowds at Apple stores around the world last week, is an attempt to reinvigorate the brand's device range and challenge the foldable phones made by rivals. An iPhone Duo user, Justin Lokos, posted an image of a device with lines across the right side of the display and later uploaded a picture of a new handset alongside the broken one, thanking an Apple store in New York for its help.

Apple boss John Ternus showing off the new iPhone Duo Android users already have a broad selection of foldable phones to choose from, technology that Apple spent years developing before the Duo's release.
