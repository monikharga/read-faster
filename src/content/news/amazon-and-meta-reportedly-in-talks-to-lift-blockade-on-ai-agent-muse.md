Amazon may be having a change of heart about Meta's AI agent, Muse. The talks would mark a significant reversal from just two months ago, when Muse users discovered they were unable to purchase products on Amazon and instead received an error message when attempting to check out. According to a Wednesday report by Fortune, citing people familiar with the discussions, Amazon and Meta have held multiple rounds of talks about the matter.

One of Amazon's biggest concerns is how third-party shopping agents like Muse could affect its advertising business. Fortune reported that executives are concerned that third-party shopping agents could weaken Amazon's direct relationship with consumers, according to people familiar with the matter. Any potential agreement between Amazon and Meta would reportedly require approval from both companies' CEOs, Fortune also reported.
