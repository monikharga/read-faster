Researchers led by Professor Jinsung Park from the Department of Biomechatronic Engineering at Sungkyunkwan University (SKKU), have developed an ultrasensitive electrochemical sensor capable of detecting extremely low concentrations of vitamin C. By incorporating artificial intelligence (AI) into the sensor development process, the research team identified promising fabrication conditions using a limited amount of experimental data, reducing the need for extensive trial-and-error optimization.
