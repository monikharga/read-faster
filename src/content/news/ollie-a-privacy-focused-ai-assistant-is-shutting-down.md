Ollie, a startup whose mission was to deliver an AI assistant that respected its users' privacy, says that it will be shutting down in a week. Bill Lennon, the company's co-founder and CEO, took to X on Friday to deliver the news, noting that the app will officially shut down on November 6. I'm grateful to our amazing team, our users, our investors, and everyone who supported us on this journey." The text-based AI assistant market has exploded over the past year, as the increasingly powerful models that undergird such applications have made them significantly more useful for daily tasks.

Crucially, Ollie was one of the first mainstream apps that managed to achieve SOC 2, a privacy compliance standard that companies can attain via an independent audit. If anything, many new digital assistants present unique privacy challenges for their users — as companies like Instinct have shown.
