Gauge theories describe how matter interacts through fields called gauge fields, which carry the electromagnetic, weak and strong forces. In lattice gauge theories, space is modeled as a grid with matter at the vertices and gauge fields on the links connecting them. "We first started brainstorming how to use a hybrid trapped-ion quantum system to simulate lattice gauge theories back in 2022.

"Gauge theories describe how particles interact with each other through gauge fields. "In this picture, the electrons are described by a matter field, while the electromagnetic field is the gauge field that mediates their interaction." Some of the most puzzling materials in physics, such as quantum spin liquids and high-temperature superconductors, are also described by gauge theories. Physicists often describe these materials using Z₂ lattice gauge theories, the simplest type, in which the field at each link of a grid can take only two values.
