As businesses increasingly turn to AI agents to automate work, the vast amounts of information locked in complex documents remain a challenge. Unsiloed AI has raised $7.5 million in seed funding led by Felicis to tackle that problem, aiming to help enterprises make their documents more accessible to AI agents through specialized technology. Unsiloed aims to solve this problem with software that breaks down complex documents, extracts information from tables, charts and other elements, and turns it into structured data that AI agents can use.

There is going to be some hallucination involved, and that has a massive negative impact downstream." Mishra co-founded Unsiloed with Adnan Abbas (pictured above, left), his former classmate at the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Kharagpur. Mishra, however, named voice AI startup Bolna AI as a customer, saying it uses Unsiloed to ingest knowledge bases for building voice agents. Businesses also sometimes use general-purpose AI models to extract information from documents, giving Unsiloed another set of competitors.
