This became especially clear after one of the two interviewees died soon after the study ended, before the first book was published. Reflexivity refers to the process by which researchers critically consider their own role in, and relationship with, their research. Previous discussions have generally focused on "prospective reflexivity," which considers how researchers influence the research process.

Drawing on my own experience, however, I turned my attention to "retrospective reflexivity," which considers how conducting research may, in turn, affect the researcher. The study is published in the journal Research Methods in Applied Linguistics. When we conduct research, we are careful about how our presence might affect a study, but many of us have tended to ignore how our studies affect us as researchers and human beings, particularly when the work spans many years.
