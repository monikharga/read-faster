Elon Musk is chief executive of both SpaceX and Tesla Elon Musk has said he is actively considering merging Tesla and SpaceX, his two biggest companies. While Musk noted it could not be assumed a merger will go ahead, and it would need shareholders' support, he said special committees within both companies had been formed to examine the move. "But I do think there is a lot of merit to merging and a lot to be gained." With Tesla valued at roughly $1.5 trillion (£1.1 trillion) and SpaceX at $2 trillion, a combination of the two companies would mark one of the largest mergers in history.

Why does Elon Musk want to merge Tesla and SpaceX? Musk, who became the world's first paper trillionaire after the SpaceX stock market debut earlier this year, said he is not included in either of the special committees considering the mega-merger. He described himself as a "conflicted party" as the chief executive of both Tesla and SpaceX.
