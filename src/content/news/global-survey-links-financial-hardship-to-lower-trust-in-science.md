Editors have highlighted the following attributes while ensuring the content's credibility: Poor access to health care, limited local influence over research priorities and an unequal distribution of scientific benefits are undermining public confidence in science, experts warn, following the release of a global trust survey. The Wellcome Global Monitor 2026 surveyed more than 140,000 people in 140 countries in 2025 and found that trust in science had fallen below prepandemic levels. Over the same period, the share reporting little or no trust in science rose from 13% in both 2018 and 2020 to 19% in 2025.

Trust was lowest among people struggling financially and higher among those who had confidence in their local health care institutions. Evelyn Gitau, chief scientific officer at the Science for Africa Foundation, who also spoke at the launch event, said, "It's people saying: My life is hard, and while I know science can fix it, it's not fixing it.
