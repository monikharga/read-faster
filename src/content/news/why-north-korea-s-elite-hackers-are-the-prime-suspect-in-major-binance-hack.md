Two major blockchain analytics firms say they have robust intelligence pointing to a North Korean state-backed group as the likely culprits in the $6.3bn (£4.7bn) hack of Binance, the world's largest cryptocurrency exchange. The Lazarus Group, a North Korean hacking unit also known as TraderTraitor, was identified "with high confidence" by TRM Labs, as well as Chainalysis. Who could be behind the Binance hack and what we know so far Crypto giant Binance attempting to recover $6.3bn stolen in hack linked to North Korea Last year, the Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI) said North Korea was responsible for the theft of $1.5bn on the Bybit exchange.

The FBI has broadly accused North Korea of "illicit cybercrime and virtual asset theft activities". North Korean hackers are also suspected in the theft of $620m worth of cryptocurrency in 2022.
