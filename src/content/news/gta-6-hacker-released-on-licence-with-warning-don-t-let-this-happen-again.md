During the trial, the prosecution described Kurtaj as a "leading light" in the teen gang Lapsus$ A cyber criminal who hacked the maker of Grand Theft Auto has been released on licence after serving the majority of a seven-year sentence. Multiple offender Arion Kurtaj, from Oxford, was released from custody with the judge warning him: "Don't let this happen again." The now 21-year-old hacked several companies and individuals, including GTA 6-maker Rockstar Games, whom he tried to extort after stealing clips of the unfinished game in 2022. With his computer confiscated, Kurtaj hacked Rockstar using an Amazon Fire Stick plugged into his hotel room television along with a Bluetooth keyboard and mouse.

He stole internal company data including 90 clips showing nearly 60 minutes of gameplay from the unfinished GTA 6, which had been shrouded in secrecy until its release last week.
